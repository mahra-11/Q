2. CORRECTION MADE ALONG THE WAY
--------------------------------
sc_dist_res1_res6 was described as "Tyr1 to Thr5". It is actually
Tyr1 to Gly6. Gly has no side chain, so the pipeline uses its
closest-heavy-atom fallback: the feature is the minimum heavy-atom
distance from Tyr1's side chain to the Gly6 BACKBONE. It measures where
Tyr1 sits relative to the beta-turn.


3. PAPERS READ IN FULL (3)
--------------------------

P1. Kang, Trizio & Parrinello, Nature Computational Science 2024
    (arXiv 2401.05279)
    Where: PDF p. 6 (Fig. 4), p. 7 (section D);
           supporting information pp. 27-29 (Figs. S19, S20, S21)
    Findings:
    - Two transition-state routes:
        TS-up:   Asp2 side chain (OD1/OD2) bonded to Thr5 N AND Thr5 OG1
                 ("bidentate")
        TS-down: backbone Asp2 N to Thr7 O ("monodentate")
    - Most important inputs: Cα distances Tyr1-Thr5 and Asp2-Thr7.
    - Turn bend (Cα Pro3 to Cα Gly6, about 0.5 nm) also forms in
      unfolded frames, so it does not separate the states.

P2. "Navigating committor landscape of biomolecules with a general
    pairwise interaction model" (arXiv 2606.31832)
    Where: main text pp. 3-4 (Fig. 3); supporting information p. 12
           (Table S1, equation S2), p. 17 (Fig. S2)
    Findings:
    - Same two routes (called TS^U and TS^D).
    - On TS^U, unfolding STARTS with Asp2 N - Thr7 O breaking, while
      Asp2 OD - Thr5 N stays intact.
    - On TS^D, the Gly6-Thr7 peptide bond flips by about 180 degrees.
    - Tyr1/Trp8 stack on TS^U; on TS^D a Tyr1 N - Trp8 O hydrogen bond
      forms instead.
    - Linear formula for the committor:
        0.53 x d(Tyr0 O - Trp8 O)
      + 0.40 x d(Asp2 O - Thr7 N)
      + 0.21 x d(Tyr1 N - Tyr9 C)
      + 0.21 x d(Tyr0 N - Thr7 O)
      + 0.12 x d(Tyr0 O - Tyr9 O)
      - 0.11 x d(Glu4 O - Tyr9 O)
      + 0.02 x d(Asp2 O - Gly6 O)

P3. Sobieraj & Setny, J. Chem. Theory Comput. 2022, 18, 1936-1944
    (Granger causality analysis)
    Where: p. 1938 (methods), p. 1940 (Figs. 2 and 3),
           pp. 1941-1942 (Fig. 5)
    Findings:
    - Uses the D.E. Shaw trajectory (534,743 frames, 0.2 ns spacing).
      Contacts are defined as shortest heavy-atom distance, the same as
      your closest-heavy approach.
    - Folding is zipper-like and driven by the turn.
    - A Tyr0-Asp2 network in the unfolded state must break first.
    - Cross-strand contacts (Tyr1-Trp8, Tyr0-Tyr9) FINISH folding
      rather than start it.
    - Fig. 3 gives how often each hydrogen bond is formed in each state.


4. THE FOLDING STORY THE PAPERS AGREE ON
---------------------------------------
Step 1. Unfolded: the N-terminal amine holds the Asp2 side chain.
Step 2. That hold breaks, and Asp2 is free.
Step 3. Transition state: Asp2 forms the turn hydrogen bonds
        (to Thr5 N and OG1, Gly6 N, Thr7 O). Which form first defines
        the two routes.
Step 4. Folded: the strands lock together (Tyr1-Trp8 packing, salt
        bridge, Tyr0 O - Tyr9 N, Tyr0 ring covering the ends).

For the model: the current top features mostly describe steps 3 and 4.
The missing information is Asp2's turn hydrogen bonds.

7. DROPPED OR LOW PRIORITY
--------------------------
- Asp2 side chain to Thr7: a duplicate of hb_2ASPN_7THRO. The original
  atom guess was wrong.
- Turn bend, Cα Pro3 to Cα Gly6: also formed in unfolded frames
  (P1 Fig. S20).
- Tyr0-Tyr9 rings: P3 found no distinct importance.
- Tyr0 ring covering the ends: real (P3 p. 1941), but it happens AFTER
  the transition state.
- Tyr1-Trp8 ring angle: mixed evidence (P2 supports it, P3 does not).
- Asp2-Glu4 intermediate turn: comes only from an unread Mitsutake
  paper (arXiv 1505.05287), which may not be about CLN025. The closest
  related bond is already in the set (hb_4GLUN_2ASPOD1/2).
  Recommended to skip.
